Music player

On this pageAdding songsSong listNow PlayingShuffle and repeatTags and cover artBackground musicProblems

Tools > Music is a PSP-style player for the songs on your card. It shows titles, artists, lengths and cover art, and the music keeps playing while you browse the launcher.

Adding songs

Copy your songs to music/ on the ROMS partition (/media/roms/music/ on the device). Sub folders are searched, hidden files and folders are skipped, and songs are listed in natural order (track2 before track10), sorted by path. The list holds up to 4000 songs.

Format Title, artist, album Length Cover art
MP3 ID3v2 (2.2, 2.3, 2.4), or ID3v1 From the Xing / VBRI header, or from bitrate and file size Yes
FLAC Vorbis comments Yes Yes
OGG / OGA (Vorbis, Opus) Vorbis comments Yes No
WAV No (file name is used) Yes No
MOD, XM, IT, S3M No (file name is used) No (shown as --:--) No

The player only reads the ROMS music/ folder. Songs bundled into the image (the repository's music/ folder) play as launcher music but do not appear in the player list.

Song list

The list opens first. Tags and lengths are read in the background, so a long list appears at once and fills in as it goes; a song without a title tag shows its file name.

Button Action
Up / Down select a song (wraps around)
L / R (or Left / Right) page up / down
A play the song and open Now Playing (on the song that is already playing it just opens Now Playing)
Start play / pause
Y shuffle on / off
X repeat: all, one, off
Select rescan the folder (shows how many songs it found)
B back to Tools

Now Playing

Cover art, title, artist, album, a progress bar with the elapsed and total time, and the shuffle and repeat state.

Button Action
A or Start play / pause
Left / Right seek 10 seconds back / forward (the jump happens when you stop pressing, so holding the button is fine)
L previous song; restarts the current one if it has played for more than 3 seconds
R next song
Y / X shuffle / repeat
B or Select back to the song list

Shuffle and repeat

  • Shuffle is off by default. When it is on, the song you picked plays first, then the rest in random order, and no song repeats until the whole list has played. A new round is shuffled again.
  • Repeat cycles all (the default), one and off. All starts over at the end of the list, one replays the current song, and off stops at the end.
  • Both are shown briefly at the bottom of the screen when you change them.

Tags and cover art

Tags and pictures are read straight from the file, nothing is downloaded. Embedded pictures (PNG or JPEG) are cropped to a square. To get cover art, embed it in the song with a tag editor; a separate cover.jpg next to the files is not used.

Background music

  • Songs keep playing when you leave the player (B back to Tools) and move on to the next one, also while the screen is off.
  • While the player is open or a song is playing, the launcher's shuffled background music stays quiet. It returns after you stop the music.
  • Music stops when a game starts.
  • Loudness follows the device's hardware volume buttons; the player itself always plays at full mixer volume.
  • The player always uses the PSP-style look, whichever launcher is selected.

Problems

Symptom Cause and fix
The list is empty Nothing playable in music/ on the ROMS partition. Copy songs there and press Select to rescan.
"Can't play <song>" or "None of the songs can be played" The file is damaged or uses a codec SDL_mixer cannot decode. Unplayable songs are skipped.
"This file cannot be searched" That format does not support seeking (some tracker modules). Play it through, or skip it with R.
No cover art The song has no embedded picture, or it is an OGG or WAV file.
Length shows --:-- The length could not be worked out from the file header (tracker modules, damaged files). The song still plays.
